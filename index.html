Machine learning

Water Potability Prediction

An end-to-end pipeline that classifies 3,276 water samples as drinkable or not from nine physicochemical measurements.

  • KNN imputation for missing pH, sulfate and trihalomethanes, chosen over mean or median to keep feature distributions.
  • EDA found weak linear relationships and a 61/39 class imbalance.
  • Random Forest raised ROC-AUC from 0.548 (logistic regression) to 0.647, tuned with GridSearchCV and 5-fold cross-validation.
  • Best model: 65.85% accuracy, F1 0.414. No single feature separates the classes well.
